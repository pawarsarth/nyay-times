Schools and colleges are exploring AI tools to make learning more interactive and accessible for students.

## What changes

Students may use AI assistants to understand difficult topics, practice questions, and receive personalized feedback.

## What happens next

Educators will need to evaluate these tools for accuracy, privacy, and their impact on student learning.
