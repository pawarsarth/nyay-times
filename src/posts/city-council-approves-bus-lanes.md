---

The council voted on Thursday to set aside one lane on each of three main roads for buses during peak hours.

## What changes

Buses will get a lane of their own from 7 to 11 a.m. and 5 to 9 p.m. Cars may use the lane outside those hours.

## What happens next

Officials said they will publish ridership and travel-time data every month during the pilot.
