Developers have a growing range of AI models to evaluate for chatbots, document search, coding assistance, and other applications. Some releases make model weights available, while others also provide code and broader rights to inspect or modify the system.

## What it means

More deployment choices can help teams test models locally or adapt them to specialised tasks. The terms “open-source” and “open-weight” are not interchangeable, and licence conditions can limit how a model may be used or redistributed.

## What happens next

Teams will need to assess model quality, hardware requirements, licensing, security, and ongoing maintenance before choosing a model for production.
