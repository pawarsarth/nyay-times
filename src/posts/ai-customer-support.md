Businesses are exploring AI assistants to answer common questions, find relevant help articles, and guide customers through basic tasks. These systems can operate alongside traditional support teams.

## What it means

Automation may shorten waiting times for simple requests, but it can also frustrate customers when an assistant misunderstands a problem. Clear escalation to a human agent is important for unusual, sensitive, or complex cases.

## What happens next

Companies will need to measure answer quality, protect customer data, and make it easy for people to reach a human when needed.
