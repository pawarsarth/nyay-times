AI features do not always need to run in a remote data centre. On-device AI processes certain tasks locally on a phone, laptop, or other compatible device.

## What it means

Local processing can reduce delays and may help limit how much personal data is sent to external servers. Performance still depends on the device's hardware, battery, memory, and the size of the AI model.

## What happens next

Device makers and developers will continue to balance speed, battery use, model quality, and privacy when deciding which AI features can run locally.
