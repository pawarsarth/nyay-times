AI translation tools can help users translate messages, web pages, documents, and spoken conversations. They can make information easier to access when people do not share the same language.

## What it means

Faster translation may help students, businesses, travellers, and online communities communicate. But idioms, cultural references, legal wording, and technical terms can be mistranslated, especially when context is limited.

## What happens next

Users should review important translations and seek qualified human support for high-stakes material such as legal, medical, or official documents.
