AI tools can be useful for drafting, summarising, coding, and analysing documents. Before sharing information, users should understand what data a service collects and how it may be stored or processed.

## What it means

Pasting passwords, API keys, private customer records, confidential company information, or personal documents into an AI tool can create avoidable risks. Available privacy controls differ across services and account types.

## What happens next

Users and organisations should review service policies, apply appropriate access controls, remove unnecessary sensitive details, and follow their workplace data-handling rules.
