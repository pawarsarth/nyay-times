AI-powered study tools are giving students new ways to review lessons and practise topics. Depending on the tool, learners may be able to ask follow-up questions, create quizzes, or receive explanations at different levels of difficulty.

## What it means

Personalised practice can help students identify topics that need more attention. However, AI answers can be incorrect, so students should compare important information with textbooks, teachers, and trusted sources.

## What happens next

Schools and students will need to develop good habits for checking accuracy, protecting personal information, and using AI to support learning rather than replace it.
