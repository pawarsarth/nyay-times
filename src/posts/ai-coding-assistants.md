AI coding assistants are becoming a common part of software development. They can suggest code, explain unfamiliar functions, and help developers explore possible solutions.

## What it means

Developers may be able to spend less time on repetitive tasks and more time reviewing designs, testing software, and solving complex problems. AI-generated code still needs careful review for correctness, security, and maintainability.

## What happens next

Teams will need practical guidelines for code review, testing, privacy, and deciding when AI suggestions are appropriate.
